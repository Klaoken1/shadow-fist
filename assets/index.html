HOW TO FIGHT

  • One hit and you are dead. No health, no second chances.
  • Tap the left or right half of the screen to strike in that direction.
  • You only hit enemies inside your range, the white marks on the ground. Swinging at air leaves you open.
  • Brutes take 3 hits. Tricksters (blue) leap over you after the first hit, so turn and strike again.
  • Strike thrown shuriken to send them back. Kill sword bearers to grab their katana.
  • When the purple meter is full, tap it for a Shadow Burst.
  • Some enemies feint just outside your range to bait a miss. Wait for them.
  • Shieldbearers (green eyes) block every strike until they lower the shield to bash. Hit them on the !
  • Bosses vanish, charge and guard. A gold glow (and gold range mark) means a strike will be blocked and you get thrown back. Wait for them to attack, or hit them mid-charge to counter.
  • A Shadow Burst breaks through any guard.
  • Spend coins in the Dojo: weapons add range, armor makes you recover from misses faster.
